Privacy Policy

Workout App · Effective October 7, 2026

This policy explains how Workout App ("the app"), made by smrtdesigns, handles your information.

Information we collect

We don't collect any personal information. The workouts, sets, reps, weights, and cardio sessions you enter are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or to any third party.

Analytics and tracking

The app doesn't use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, and it doesn't share data with data brokers or advertisers.

Device backups

If you use iCloud or computer backups, your app data may be included in those backups, which are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information, it doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

You can delete all app data at any time by deleting the app from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
