Frequently asked questions

Where is my workout data stored?
All of your workout data is stored on your device. We don't collect it or send it to any server.
Will I lose my data if I delete the app?
Yes. Deleting the app removes its data from your device. Data may be included in your device backups, depending on your backup settings.
How do I report a bug?
Email us with a short description of what happened, your iPhone model, and your iOS version. Screenshots help a lot.
