Privacy Policy

Rackside · Effective October 7, 2026

This policy explains how Rackside ("the app"), made by smrtdesigns, handles your information.

Information we collect

We don't collect any personal information. The workouts, sets, reps, weights, and cardio sessions you enter are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or to any third party. The only exception is voice input, described below.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you choose to use voice features, the app asks for permission to use your microphone and speech recognition so you can control your workout hands-free. Your speech is converted to text using Apple's built-in speech recognition. Depending on your device and settings, Apple may process this audio on its servers. That processing is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

We don't record, store, or receive your audio or transcripts. Voice input is used only in the moment to carry out your command. Any spoken responses from the app are generated on your device.

Voice features are optional. You can turn off microphone or speech recognition access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security on your device.

Analytics and tracking

The app doesn't use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, and it doesn't share data with data brokers or advertisers.

Device backups

If you use iCloud or computer backups, your app data may be included in those backups, which are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information, it doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

You can delete all app data at any time by deleting the app from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
